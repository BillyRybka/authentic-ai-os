# Credibility Line Weaving

How `vid-intro` decides WHICH of the 3 intro slots (Hook, Problem/Result, Setup) the credibility line weaves into, given the credibility form available. The 5 credibility forms (Vast experience, Volume of people helped, Big personal result, Big client result, Effort signal) and the bolted-on-vs-woven failure mode all live in [[intro-architecture]] Step 6. This file does NOT restate either. It teaches the form-to-slot mapping decision and gives runtime calibration examples for each combination.

Examples-first contrastive.

## Where to weave (the 3 slot decision)

Credibility weaves into ONE of three slots: Hook, Problem/Result, Setup. Never as a standalone fourth beat. Pick the slot based on which form fits.

**Slot 1: Hook**

- Best for: Big personal result, Big client result, Vast experience (when the experience is the entire engine of the video, e.g. interview-shaped intros where the GUEST'S credibility is the hook)
- Worked: "I've been writing daily for 12 years and built three businesses to seven figures off it." (Cr-1, Big personal result + Vast experience blended into the Hook itself)
- Near-miss: "Hi, I've been writing for 12 years. Today we're talking about writing." (same numbers, but no claim is being made, so the credibility floats)

**Slot 2: Problem/Result**

- Best for: Big client result, Big personal result, Volume of people helped (when the receipt IS the result you're teasing)
- Worked: "Do you hate making thumbnails? I used to until I figured out a simple thumbnail formula that generated millions of views and $6 million from YouTube in just two years." (Tease-the-Result with the $6M baked in as proof of the formula's power)
- Near-miss: "Do you hate making thumbnails? I made $6 million on YouTube." (the $6M is just stated, not tied to the formula being teased; viewer gets credibility but no curiosity hook into the rest of the video)

**Slot 3: Setup**

- Best for: Effort signal, Volume of people helped (when the credibility frames why the methodology in the body is trustworthy)
- Worked: "So in this video, after testing this on 200 clients with the same outcome every time, I'm going to show you the three moves that flipped their week-six retention." (Volume of people helped woven into the Setup so the methodology arrives pre-validated)
- Near-miss: "I've used this on 200 clients. Anyway, in this video, I'm going to show you three moves." (the "anyway" pivot tells the viewer the credibility is filler, not proof)

## Worked: Big personal result woven into Problem/Result (Combine option)

**Brain dump material:** creator made $90k by changing one thumbnail across their channel. Avatar problem #1 is "my videos don't get clicks."

**Output:**

> "Do you hate making thumbnails? Every creator I know does, because they take so long and they rarely work. The thing is, your thumbnail is the only ad your video gets. Fixing mine made me $90,000 in four months. So now there's a formula I want to give you so you can do the same."

**Why this lands:**

- Pokes the avatar's problem ("hate making thumbnails")
- Ties the cost ("they rarely work" + "the only ad your video gets") to the reason for fixing it
- Slips the $90k receipt INTO the moment the claim about the formula gets made; the receipt earns the formula
- Closes with the tease ("formula I want to give you")

The credibility never sits on its own. It earns the rest of the intro.

## Near-miss: Same material, bolted-on

**Wrong:**

> "Hi, I made $90,000 fixing thumbnails over four months. In this video, I'm going to show you my thumbnail formula. Do you hate making thumbnails?"

**Why this fails:**

- The $90k arrives BEFORE any claim about thumbnails being valuable. Cold viewer reads it as a brag, not a proof
- The "do you hate making thumbnails?" comes AFTER the credibility, so the problem-poke loses its energy (the viewer is already evaluating the creator)
- The Setup ("show you my formula") doesn't tie to the receipt. The receipt and the formula float independently

Same numbers. Different placement. The bolted version reads as resume; the woven version reads as proof.

## Worked: Effort signal in Setup for a Deep Dive

**Brain dump material:** creator analyzed 247 sales pages this month. Found three patterns. Avatar problem #2 is "my sales page doesn't convert."

**Output:**

> "Most coaches' sales pages convert at under 1%. The thing is, the difference between a 1% page and a 6% page isn't copy length or design. It's three structural moves nobody teaches. So I'm going to show you those three moves, after analyzing 247 sales pages this month and finding the exact pattern that separates the winners from the losers."

**Why this lands:**

- Effort signal ("analyzed 247 sales pages this month") sits inside the Setup, framing the methodology as research-backed
- The framing tells the viewer "this isn't theory, it's pattern-matching across real data"
- Curiosity stays alive because the actual three moves haven't been revealed yet; the credibility earns the wait

## Near-miss: Effort signal in Setup that floats

**Wrong:**

> "I analyzed 247 sales pages. In this video, I'm going to show you three moves to fix your sales page conversion."

**Why this fails:**

- The 247 number arrives unanchored. The viewer reads "OK, you analyzed pages, and?" because the connection to the three moves isn't made
- Setup lists the deliverable ("three moves") but doesn't tie back to why the analysis matters
- The viewer mentally separates the credibility (research) from the value (three moves), which means the credibility doesn't earn the value

The fix in the worked version: phrase the sentence so the analysis is the SOURCE of the three moves, not a separate brag.

## Worked: Big client result woven into Hook (Case Study format)

**Brain dump material:** Steve, a Case Study client, went from 0 clients to $80k/month in six months.

**Output:**

> "Six months ago Steve hadn't landed a single client. Now he's doing $80k a month. Same niche, same offer, same skill level. Three things changed."

**Why this lands:**

- Hook IS the credibility. The receipt itself ("0 to $80k/month in 6 months") is the engine of the video
- The implied question ("how?") is what pulls the viewer through
- "Three things changed" doubles as a Setup tease, no separate Setup line needed

This is the pattern Case Study intros default to: the receipt up front as both Hook AND credibility. Other formats can use this shape when the receipt is loud enough to earn the cold viewer's attention by itself.

## Near-miss: Big client result that's wrong-stage for the avatar

**Wrong:**

> "Six months ago Sarah was making $250k a month. Now she's making $1M a month. Three things changed."

**Why this fails (for an avatar making $10k/month):**

- Same shape, same energy, but the relatability collapses
- The viewer's brain reads "$250k to $1M is a different planet from where I am" and tunes out
- The credibility lands on the creator, but the viewer doesn't see themselves in the case

**The rule:** match the case to the avatar's stage. Big client results only earn the Hook slot when the avatar can mentally simulate themselves in the client's starting state. If the avatar is at $10k, use a $0-to-$15k case, not a $250k-to-$1M case. Pulled from `format-planners/case-study.md` Story Rules.

## Worked: Volume of people helped, woven into Problem/Result for Roast

**Brain dump material:** creator has reviewed 200+ thumbnails for clients. Found three universal mistakes.

**Output:**

> "Today we're fixing three thumbnails. The thing is, after reviewing 200+ of these, the same three mistakes show up in 80% of them, and they're invisible until you know what to look for. So we're going to find what's wrong with each, fix them, and give you a way to apply this to your own."

**Why this lands:**

- Volume signal ("200+ of these") earns the diagnostic claim ("same three mistakes show up")
- The "invisible until you know what to look for" line creates curiosity for the body
- Setup ties review to viewer's own application

## When credibility woven sits in the HOOK vs the PROBLEM/RESULT vs the SETUP

A simple decision flow:

- **Receipt dramatic enough to stop a cold viewer in their tracks?** → Hook (Case Study, Deep Dive, sometimes Listicle)
- **Receipt is proof of the result you're teasing?** → Problem/Result (Tease or Combine)
- **Credibility frames the methodology in the body?** → Setup (Effort signal or Volume helped)

Default to Problem/Result if unsure. That's the most common slot across formats because that's where claims earn it.

## When NO credibility line is needed

Some intros earn attention without weaving credibility at all:

- **News:** the news IS the value. Cold viewers came for the story, not the creator
- **Roast (small channel):** the visual material (the contestant submission on screen) carries the energy
- **Short Process (small channel):** the result IS the credibility most of the time

When the format planner says credibility is "often skipped," skip it. Don't force a weave just to follow a template. The format planner knows when the format earns trust without it.

## What this file does NOT do

- It does not enforce a credibility weave on every intro. Some formats skip it.
- It does not pick the credibility content (the actual numbers, names, results). That comes from `foundation/credibility.md` or the brain dump's lock list.
- It does not generate the entire Hook or Problem/Result. It tells the skill where to put the credibility once the slot's draft is in hand.
