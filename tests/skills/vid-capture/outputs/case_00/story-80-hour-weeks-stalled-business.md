# The two years I out-worked my own growth

## Problem

I worked 80 hour weeks for two years and my business did not grow. It stayed the same while making me a worse overall person.

## Action

I stopped adding hours and built a system for client onboarding. I realized I was doing the same thing over and over again, and I needed to start to build systems.

## Outcome

The business started growing without me pushing harder. The goal is not to do more, the goal is to build the thing that does it for you.

> [!tip] Why this story lands
> The honest cost is the hook: it stayed the same while making me a worse overall person. Two years of 80 hour weeks with nothing to show is the pain every hustler recognizes.
