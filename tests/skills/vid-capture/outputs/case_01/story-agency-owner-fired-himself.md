# The agency owner who fired himself from onboarding

## Problem

[[Marcus Lane]] ran a small content agency and he was the bottleneck on everything. Nothing shipped unless he touched it. Onboarding a new client ate five hours of his week, every time, because the whole process lived in his head.

## Action

We spent six weeks pulling the onboarding out of his head and into a documented system. Every step written down, an owner assigned, a checklist his coordinator could run without him.

## Outcome

He handed it to his coordinator and stopped being the bottleneck. His own onboarding time dropped from five hours to one.

> [!tip] Why this story lands
> Delegation is not about trust, it is about documentation. He was not the only one who could do the work, he was just the only one with the steps in his head.
