# The leaky cup

## Concept being clarified

A process that only lives in your head leaks every time you try to hand it off. Written down, it holds.

## The metaphor

### Spoken

"A process in your head is this cup. Every time you try to hand it off, it leaks. Written down, it holds every drop."

### Shown

Two paper cups on the table. The cup labeled "memory" has a hole poked in the bottom and water drips onto the table while the creator talks. The cup labeled "written down" stays full.

## When it lands best

When the viewer keeps re-explaining the same process to their team and calls it delegation.

> [!tip] The pivot phrase
> "So what is still in the leaky cup in your business?"
