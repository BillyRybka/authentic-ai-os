# The Handoff Ladder

## What problem does this solve?

Delegation falls apart when the handoff has no structure. The owner says "I delegated it" and a week later the work is back on their desk. The ladder gives every handoff three rungs so nothing slides back.

## The components

1. **Write it down:** the process leaves your head and becomes a document. Until it is written, you have not delegated, you have just hoped.
2. **Hand it off:** a named owner takes the document and runs it. One owner, not a group.
3. **Review it weekly:** a standing check so the system drifts back into shape instead of back onto your desk.

## The shape

Arrows. Strict order, you cannot skip a rung. You cannot hand off what is not written, and you cannot review what was never handed off.

## When to use it

- Videos about delegation failing or the owner becoming the bottleneck again
- Any segment teaching how to hand a process to a team member
