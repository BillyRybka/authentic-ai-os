# Pattern Bank (vid-ideas TEST FIXTURE)

Synthetic outlier set for the test creator's niche (solo service business
owners). Built so the vid-ideas eval has per-channel raw rows to trace receipts
against. Every channel, title, view count, and multiple is invented for the
eval; nothing here is a real creator or a real video. The @agenticalex circle
is the deliberate off-lane trap: huge numbers, wrong audience.

## Synthesis (cross-channel insights)

The "what we learned" layer. vid-ideas reads this for orientation, then works
from the raw per-channel rows below.

> [!important] Convergent patterns (proven across the most channels)
> 1. **Speed-to-outcome compression** `[Process] in [N] Minutes`. Olivia, Dan, Pat. ON-LANE.
> 2. **First-person firing story** `I Fired Myself From [Role] (...)`. Dan, plus Sam's own "I fired myself" hook. ON-LANE.
> 3. **Superiority stat** `[N]% of [Avatar] [Does X] Wrong`. Olivia. ON-LANE.
> 4. **Named-system receipt** `The [Name] System That [Outcome]` / `(Full System)`. Olivia, Pat. ON-LANE.

> [!note] Niche-specific patterns (strong for YOUR audience)
> 1. **Zero-employees agency** `I Run a $[N]k a Month [Business] With Zero Employees (...)`. Olivia. Speaks to the owner who wears every hat.
> 2. **Price-increase receipt** `I Raised My Prices [N]% and Lost Zero Clients`. Pat. Direct answer to problem 3.
> 3. **Misdiagnosed problem** `You Do Not Have a [Surface] Problem (You Have a [Real] Problem)`. Ownerbrain. Convergent with Sam's own iceberg.

> [!warning] Off-lane circles (mine shape only, never topics)
> @agenticalex prints views with make-money-with-AI topics (`$100k in 30 Days`,
> `Prints Money While You Sleep`). That audience is not Sam's audience and the
> topics sit outside the iceberg. Do not bend ideas toward these topics. A
> shape may transfer only onto a systems, delegation, pricing, or mindset
> topic; the topic itself never transfers.
