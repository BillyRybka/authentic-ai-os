# The Boring System That Grew Jordan's Business

## Intro
A client named Jordan runs a coaching business. We built out their systems and it grew a ton. It was honestly wild how fast it moved. I want to walk you through exactly what we built, because the growth came from the boring system, not from a growth hack, and that is the part nobody wants to hear.

## Setup
**Parable:** story. Jordan, the coaching business, everything routing through one head. No bank entry captured yet.
**Principle:** stakes first; a business that works but is held together by memory.
Jordan came in with a coaching business that worked but creaked. Clients came in, delivery happened, and everything important routed through Jordan's head. The work was good. The business was held together by memory. So we built two things: the documented onboarding, and a weekly review. That is the whole build. What happened next is the part I did not fully believe until I watched it happen.

## Problem
**Parable:** story (continued). Growth was stuck because every system was a person.
**Principle:** memory does not scale; documents do.

## Action
**Parable:** story (continued). We built the documented onboarding plus a weekly review.
**Principle:** two boring parts, run every week, no exceptions.

## Outcome
**Parable:** story. It grew a ton, honestly wild how fast it moved.
