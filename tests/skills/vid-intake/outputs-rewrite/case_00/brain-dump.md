## Raw dump (verbatim)

I want to make a video about why systems beat hustle. Everyone in my world thinks the answer to being slammed is to wake up earlier and grind harder. That is the trap. I worked 80 hour weeks for two years and my business did not grow. It stayed the same while making me a worse overall person. What actually changed was that I built a system for client onboarding. I realized I was doing the same thing over and over again, and I needed to start building systems. I want to show people that the goal is not to do more, the goal is to build the thing that does it for you.
