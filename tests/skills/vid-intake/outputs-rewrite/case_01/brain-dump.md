## Raw dump (verbatim)

I want to do a video on a client win. Marcus ran a small agency and he was the bottleneck on everything. Nothing shipped unless he touched it. We spent six weeks pulling the onboarding out of his head and into a documented system, and he handed it to his coordinator. He cut his own onboarding time from 5 hours to 1 hour and he stopped being the bottleneck. The teaching point is that delegation is not about trust, it is about documentation.

That's most of it. Marcus is a real client, he runs a content agency. I've got his story and the proof screenshot in my banks already. The shift for him was when he realized he wasn't the only one who could do the work, he was just the only one with the steps in his head.
