# Script: How Marcus Cut Client Onboarding From 5 Hours to 1

## Intro

Marcus cut his client onboarding from 5 hours to 1 by firing himself from his own process.

> [!visual-proof] Put the time-log screenshot on screen at "5 hours to 1": [[proof-bank/onboarding-5h-to-1h]]

Six weeks earlier, nothing in his agency shipped unless he touched it. He was the bottleneck on everything. The fix was not trust. It was documentation, and the way we built it is the part most owners get backwards.

> [!note] Full story beats live in [[story-bank/agency-owner-fired-himself]] for segment 1.

So in this video, I am going to show you where Marcus started, what the six weeks of documentation actually looked like, and how to run the same play on the one task only you can do.

So let me take you back to where this started, because the bottleneck was worse than it sounds.

## Segment 1: Where Marcus started

- purpose: establish the starting state so the receipt lands
- key material: Marcus ran a small content agency and he was the bottleneck on everything, nothing shipped unless he touched it; [[story-bank/agency-owner-fired-himself]]
- transition out: so we spent six weeks pulling the whole process out of his head

## Segment 2: The six weeks

- purpose: show the documentation work, step by step
- key material: every step written down, an owner assigned, a checklist his coordinator could run without him
- transition out: then came the handoff

## Segment 3: The handoff

- purpose: the receipt beat
- key material: he handed it to his coordinator and cut his own onboarding time from 5 hours to 1 hour; [[proof-bank/onboarding-5h-to-1h]]
- transition out: which is the part most owners get backwards

## Segment 4: The teaching point

- purpose: land the lesson and the viewer assignment
- key material: delegation is not about trust, it is about documentation; pick the one task only you can do right now and document it
- transition out: into the ending CTA
