The hook cannot open on Jordan. Per hooks.md: a story opens on the reader's tension or the surprising claim, then brings the character in as proof. "Jordan runs a coaching business" as line one is a subject-facing flat opening. Strip it. Open on the claim that implicates the reader's assumption.

Strong opener: "The fastest growth I have ever seen in a client's business came from the most boring system we could build." Faces the reader via a contradictory claim. Opens the gap immediately. No warm-up.
