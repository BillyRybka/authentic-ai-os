# Delegation is documentation (Marcus onboarding)

## Core

Marcus ran a small content agency and he was the bottleneck on everything. Nothing shipped unless he touched it.

Onboarding a new client was five hours of his week, every time. Not because the work was hard. Because every step of it lived in his head.

We spent six weeks pulling the onboarding out of his head and writing every step down. An owner assigned to each one. A checklist his coordinator could run without calling Marcus.

He handed it off. His onboarding time dropped from five hours to one. He stopped being the bottleneck.

The thing he kept saying afterward was that it was not a trust problem. He trusted his coordinator the whole time. The problem was that his coordinator had no way to do the work without him, because the steps only existed in Marcus's head.

Delegation does not fail because you cannot trust people. It fails because you never wrote the steps down.

Pick the one task right now that only you can do. Write down how you do it, every step, like you are handing it to someone for the first time. That document is the handoff. Trust has nothing to do with it.
