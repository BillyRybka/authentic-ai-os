**Post Writer (core piece):**

---

If your onboarding is eating your week, the process is not broken. It is missing.

Here are the five gaps I see most often:

1. No written steps. It all lives in your head. Which means it has to be you, every time.
2. Client onboarding and team onboarding treated as the same thing. They are not the same job. One is about helping the client feel they made the right choice. The other is about getting your team up to speed. Collapsing them into one frantic week makes both worse.
3. No owner. A process with no owner defaults back to you. I had a client who had actually built out his onboarding but never assigned it to anyone. Every new client, it landed on him anyway.
4. Documented once, never updated. Processes drift. The work changes, the tools change, the scope changes. If nobody is updating the document when the work updates, the team stops trusting it and starts asking you.
5. You measure nothing. If you do not know how long it takes or where clients get stuck, you cannot tell if it is working. You are just repeating the same week and hoping.

Mistake one drives all the others. You cannot assign a process that is not written down. You cannot update it. You cannot measure it. Fix that first.

Audit your onboarding against these five. Find the first one that is broken and fix it this week.
