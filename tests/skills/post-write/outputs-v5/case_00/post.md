# Systems beat hustle (contrarian)

## Core

Waking up earlier is not the answer to being slammed.

I ran 80-hour weeks for two years. My business did not grow. It stayed the same while making me a worse overall person.

The problem was not my effort. The problem was that everything ran through me. The work only lived in my head, so it had to be me every single time.

What actually changed was not a better schedule. I built a system for client onboarding. I realized I was doing the same thing over and over, wrote the steps down, and handed it off.

Think about a great restaurant kitchen on a busy night. It is loud, fast, and calm at the same time. Not because the chef is moving faster than everyone else. Because every station knows its job without being told. The chef built the system, then stepped back.

Right now you are the chef sprinting between every station and calling that hard work. It is not hard work. It is a missing system.

The goal is not to do more. The goal is to build the thing that does it for you.

This week: pick the one task you do over and over. Write every step down. That is the first move out of the bottleneck.
