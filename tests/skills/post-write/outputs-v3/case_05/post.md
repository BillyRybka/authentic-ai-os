# Jordan coaching business: boring system is the growth lever

## Core

The fastest growth I have ever seen in a client's business came from the most boring system we could build.

Not a new offer. Not a viral hook. Not a rebrand.

Documented onboarding and a weekly review.

Jordan runs a coaching business. When we started, the work was all in their head. Nothing was written down. Every new client kicked off the same scramble, and the weekly check-in existed only when Jordan remembered to do it.

We spent a few weeks pulling both of those out of Jordan's head and into a written process. Onboarding became a document anyone could run. The weekly review became a standing block with a checklist.

It was honestly wild how fast it moved after that.
