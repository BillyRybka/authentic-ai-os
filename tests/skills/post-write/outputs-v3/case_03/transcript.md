**ASSISTANT:**

Here is the core. Read it and tell me if this is your material and sounds like you.

---

If your onboarding keeps pulling you back in, it is not because the work is complicated.

It is because the process has one of these five gaps.

1. No written steps. It all lives in your head, which means it has to be you every time.

2. Client onboarding and team onboarding treated as the same thing. They are two different jobs. One is about making the client feel good. The other is about getting your team ready to deliver. Mixing them into one messy week makes both worse.

3. No owner. A process with no owner defaults back to you. I had a client who had actually built out his onboarding but never assigned it to anyone. Every new client, it landed on him anyway.

4. Documented once, never updated. The process drifts. The work changes, the tools change, the scope changes. If no one is updating the document when the work updates, the team stops trusting it and starts asking you instead.

5. You measure nothing so you cannot tell if it is working. No time tracked, no feedback loop. You are repeating the same week and hoping.

Mistake one is the big one. You cannot assign a process that is not written down. You cannot update it or measure it either. Fix that first.

Audit your onboarding against these five. Find the first one that is broken and fix it this week.
