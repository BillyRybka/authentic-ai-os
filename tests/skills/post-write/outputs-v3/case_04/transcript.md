## Phase 3: Classify post-type and pick target platforms

**Post Writer (internal):**

What is the idea's job?

"People undercharge and it kills them" can read two ways. It challenges a belief (undercharging feels safe, this says it is not). And it protects from a costly move already in progress. Running the rubric:

- Is it correcting a behavior they are doing wrong? Yes, undercharging is the behavior.
- Is it protecting the reader from a costly move? Yes, "it kills them" is the cost signal.
- Is it challenging a belief they hold? Yes, low prices feel like low risk. That is the wrong belief.

The job lens: the idea has to WARN and CONVINCE. The primary driver is that the reader is already making this mistake and it is costing them, and they cannot see it as a mistake. That is a warning shape: name the move, show the cost, give the safer path.

But the proof slot is empty. A warning without a specific cost is thinner. The belief-flip (contrarian) can work from the claim alone because it just needs to name the belief and flip it. Warning needs to show the cost, which is the very gap here.
