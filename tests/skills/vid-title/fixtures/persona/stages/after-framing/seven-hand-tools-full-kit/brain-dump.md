## Raw dump (verbatim)

People keep asking what to buy first. Wrong question. I build every piece in my shop with 7 hand tools. Seven. The No. 4 hand plane flattens every board I touch. The 12-inch backsaw cuts all my joinery. The marking knife, a set of chisels, a card scraper, a mallet, a coping saw. That is the whole kit. No table saw in this garage. I want to walk through all 7, show the one job each tool owns, and hand the kit over so a beginner can steal it, stop tool shopping, and start building this weekend. You do not need a table saw. You need these 7 tools and a pile of reps.
