## Raw dump (verbatim)

Everyone thinks the move is save up, buy the table saw, build out the dream shop, then the good furniture comes. That is backwards. My neighbor Dana asked me to teach her in March. She had never touched a hand plane. Her first weeks were brutal. She ruined 11 boards learning to flatten stock and cut joinery. Eleven. We counted them and kept them in a pile. Ninety days after her first cut she sold her first piece, a trestle table, for $2,400. Her whole kit is 4 tools. No table saw, not even a borrowed one. What changed was not her shop. What changed was the reps. The ruined boards are the tuition. You do not need a dream shop. You need a small kit, sharp edges, and the next rep.

## Topic + angle

You do not need a dream shop to build furniture that sells. Dana went from never touching a hand plane to a $2,400 trestle table in 90 days with 4 tools and 11 ruined boards. The dream shop is procrastination dressed as planning. The reps are the lever.
