# Packaging System

Nora's starting video defaults, authored from the first pattern-research build ([[pattern-bank]]). These are useful first choices, not final rules. The channel has no published winners on the current positioning yet, so everything here is a starting bet. Update it as `vid-research`, `vid-framing`, and post-publish data give stronger evidence.

## Starting Packaging Defaults

*The three layers of every video, lined up to the avatar (the weekend woodworker who owns more tools than finished pieces).*

### Thumbnail style (wrapping paper)

- **Style:** Cognitive Dissonance as the lead, a contrarian line plus Nora's face or a sharp hand-tool prop, with bold clean type. Result photos (the finished piece, the counted ruined boards) layered on as proof, not as the whole play.
- **Evidence basis:** pattern-bank research (the direct channels, especially [[@wrenhalloran]]'s contrarian thumbnail identity).
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Watch for:** CTR of contrarian thumbnails vs clean before-and-after photos once real videos publish.

### Format preference (box)

- **Format:** Short Process opens most often (a clear step-by-step to one finished beginner build).
- **Evidence basis:** pattern-bank research + `format-rotation-guide` avatar fit.
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Watch for:** retention drop mid-process means the steps are too dense.

### Content type (gift)

- **Content type:** hand-tool builds and honest tool guidance, proof-backed. What the avatar keeps coming back for is "show me the small kit and the reps, and prove a beginner actually finished."
- **Evidence basis:** creator foundation (iceberg + pillars) + pattern-bank topic clusters.
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Watch for:** whether the audience pulls harder on projects (build this) or skills (get good at this).

## Starting Format Rotation (3+1)

*Three core formats to rotate, plus one experimental slot every 4th video.*

### Core Format 1
- **Format:** Short Process (10-20 min)
- **Why this is a good first test:** the avatar wants a clear step-by-step path, and the proven winners in Nora's lane are exactly this ("How I Cut Perfect Dovetails With a $14 Backsaw," Tom's one-sheet builds). Repeatable solo.
- **Evidence basis:** pattern-bank research (direct channels)
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Watch for:** if a specific outcome isn't named, it drifts to vague "how to improve" and stalls.

### Core Format 2
- **Format:** Listicle (10-20 min)
- **Why this is a good first test:** accessible entry for cold viewers, batchable, and proven ("11 Hand Tools I Can't Live Without," "7 Finishing Mistakes That Make Pine Look Cheap").
- **Evidence basis:** pattern-bank research (direct + adjacent)
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Watch for:** going shallow. Each item needs a real payoff, not filler.

### Core Format 3
- **Format:** Case Study (10-30 min)
- **Why this is a good first test:** Nora has the proof most small channels don't (her student Dana's 11 ruined boards to a $2,400 table in 90 days). Highest-trust format, and trust is the whole positioning.
- **Evidence basis:** creator proof + `format-rotation-guide` (Case Study scores high on trust)
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Watch for:** needs a real Problem-Action-Outcome arc, not just a result stated.

### Experimental Slot (every 4th video)
- **Format to test:** News (5-15 min)
- **Hypothesis:** covering tool releases ("[Tool] Just Dropped") pulls new viewers in a gear-obsessed niche. Proven pattern in the adjacent tool-review circle.
- **Evidence basis:** pattern-bank research (news-jack-release, spread 2 of 12)
- **Confidence:** experimental
- **Watch for:** promote into core only if it beats baseline across 2-3 videos AND brings the right viewer. Do not let the channel become tool-news dependent, that is the tool-collecting trap in content form.

> [!note] Formats deliberately left out at first build
> Roast (no submissions pipeline yet), Deep Dive (too long for a cold channel), Shop Tour (reads as tool collecting, off-iceberg). Revisit as the channel earns trust.

## Thumbnail Strategy Test Plan

*Two primary strategies to test first, plus two active tools to layer on. Give each enough reps before judging.*

### Strategy 1
- **Strategy:** Cognitive Dissonance (contrarian)
- **Why this is a good first test:** it's Nora's voice in a thumbnail (against the dream-shop default) and it dominates the direct channels. The highest-fit strategy in the build.
- **Evidence basis:** pattern-bank research ([[@wrenhalloran]], [[@maplemaineco]])
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Watch for:** whether contrarian lines beat clean finished-piece photos on CTR.

### Strategy 2
- **Strategy:** Result / Before-and-After
- **Why this is a good first test:** the ruined-boards-to-finished-piece arc is the niche's trust engine. Anchored to proof Nora can actually stand behind (Dana's counted boards and first sale).
- **Evidence basis:** pattern-bank research ([[@twoboardtom]], [[@salvageandsaw]]) + creator proof
- **Confidence:** medium (low until more first-sale stories land)
- **Watch for:** never a fabricated result. A made-up number is the exact slop this brand fights.

### Active enhancers (layer on either strategy)
- **Counted proof:** the literal pile of ruined boards, the tally on the wall. Nobody else shows the failures, so the failures are the thumb-stopper.
- **Minimal type-led:** the clean fallback (bold type + one strong visual), proven by [[@coppercreekshop]].

## Packaging One-Pager Checklist

*Apply to every video before posting.*

- [ ] Title under ~50 characters
- [ ] Title uses a shape from [[title-bank]] (and at least one [[power-words-bank]] word that fits)
- [ ] Thumbnail leads with one current test strategy (Cognitive Dissonance or Result/Before-and-After)
- [ ] Title and thumbnail make the SAME promise (read as one unit, not two)
- [ ] Thumbnail text 5 words or fewer
- [ ] Format matches the rotation (Short Process / Listicle / Case Study, or the News experiment)
- [ ] The promise matches what the video actually delivers (no slop, no fabricated numbers)
