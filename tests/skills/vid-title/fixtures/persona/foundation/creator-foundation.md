# Creator Foundation (Nora Beck, Beck Builds)

> [!warning] Synthetic test persona, invented for the eval
> Nora Beck, her channel, her students, and every number below are fictional, built only to exercise the vid-title eval on a niche far from the repo owner's own. Nothing here is a real creator. The channel-agnostic rule: no real channel content enters test data, so this persona stands in for one.

## Iceberg statement

The dream shop is procrastination dressed as planning. Most beginners believe the shop makes the furniture: one more tool, one more upgrade, then they will finally start. It is backwards. Skill comes from reps with a small kit that stays sharp, and confidence comes from finishing pieces, not collecting machines. The beginners who end up building real furniture are the ones who start with what they have, ruin some boards, and finish anyway.

## Avatar

The weekend woodworker with a garage, a small budget, and a saved folder of dream projects. They have watched hundreds of build videos, they own more tools than finished pieces, and they are quietly scared of making the first cut on good lumber because they know they might ruin it.

## Top 3 problems (in viewer language)

1. **I keep buying tools instead of building, and I still have not finished one piece.** Tool shopping feels like progress. It is not. The garage fills up and the build list stays a list.
2. **I am scared I will ruin expensive wood, so I never start.** Every project waits for "when I am better," and better never comes because the reps never happen.
3. **My garage is full of half-finished builds and I do not know how to get them done.** Starting is exciting. Finishing is a skill, and nobody teaches the last ten percent.

## Content pillars

- Beginner builds that finish (small, real, done)
- Hand-tool fundamentals (the small kit, kept sharp)
- Honest tool guidance (what you actually need, and what can wait)
- Finishing that sells (the last ten percent that makes a piece look pro)
