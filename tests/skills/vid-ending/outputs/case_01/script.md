# My Client Fired Himself From Onboarding

## Intro

Marcus ran a small content agency and he was the bottleneck on everything. Nothing shipped unless he touched it. In this video I will walk you through how we spent six weeks pulling the onboarding out of his head and into a documented system, and how he cut his own onboarding time from 5 hours to 1 hour. The teaching point: delegation is not about trust, it is about documentation.

## The bottleneck

Every client onboarding ran through Marcus. He was proud of being the one who could do the work, and that pride is exactly what kept him stuck. If he took a week off, the business stopped.

## Six weeks of documentation

We spent six weeks pulling the onboarding out of his head. Every step written down, every decision captured, and then handed to his coordinator. The shift was when he realized he was not the only one who could do the work, he was just the only one with the steps in his head.

## The result

He cut his own onboarding time from 5 hours to 1 hour and he stopped being the bottleneck. Your move: pick the one task only you can do right now and document it so it is no longer only you.
