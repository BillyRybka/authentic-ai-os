# The Boring System Behind Jordan's Growth

## Intro

So this thing happened with a client named Jordan. Jordan runs a coaching business. We built out their systems and it grew a ton, it was honestly wild how fast it moved. In this video I will tell that story as a case study, and you will see that the growth came from the boring system, not from a growth hack.

## What we built

The system we built was the documented onboarding plus a weekly review. Nothing clever. Every step of the onboarding written down so someone else could run it, and one weekly review to keep it honest.

## Why it worked

The growth came from the boring system, not from a growth hack. Once the onboarding ran without Jordan in the room, delivery stopped eating the week, and the business had room to grow.
