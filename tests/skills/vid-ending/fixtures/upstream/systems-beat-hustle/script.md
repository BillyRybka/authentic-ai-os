# Why Working Harder Is Keeping You Stuck

## Intro

I worked 80 hour weeks for two years and my business did not grow. It stayed the same while making me a worse overall person. Everyone around me said the answer was to wake up earlier and grind harder. That is the trap. In this video I will show you why systems beat hustle, and how to build the thing that does the work for you. By the end you will pick one repeatable task this week and write the steps down so someone else can run it.

## The hustle trap

The answer to being slammed is not more hours. I tried that. Two years of 80 hour weeks, and the business did not move, because the work that feels like it has to be you almost never does. More hours makes the business heavier, not bigger.

## The onboarding system

What actually changed was that I built a system for client onboarding. I was doing the same thing over and over again. My client Marcus cut his onboarding from 5 hours per client down to 1 hour just by writing the steps down and handing them off. Same work, same quality, no heroics.

## The kitchen, not the chef

A good kitchen runs because every station knows its job, not because the chef is faster than everyone else. Your business works the same way. The goal is not to do more. The goal is to build the thing that does it for you.
