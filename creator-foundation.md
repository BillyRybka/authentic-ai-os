## Offer

*What the creator currently sells or plans to sell. One paragraph. The result the buyer walks away with.*

> The **Authentic AI Inner Circle** is a year-long group coaching program for business owners who want to use AI to amplify their expertise through content (YouTube first, then Instagram and beyond) without the AI slop. The promise: produce content faster, sharper, and better, while still sounding like the creator, so the audience keeps trusting them as the expert. AI amplifies expertise, it does not replace it. The reason this matters: once the audience can tell content is AI, trust drops, they disengage, and depending on how long it has been going on, the brand can take a hit that is not fixable.

## Avatar

*Who this is for. A description, not a structured field list. A few sentences.*

> A six-figure business owner running a personal brand. Solo or with a contractor / small team, but the channel is theirs, in their name. They know they should be making content, they believe in it, but creating it on top of running the business feels like a full-time job they can't sustain. They go in cycles: consistent for a bit, then fall off because client work takes over, then come back. They want to use AI to help, but everything they try sounds like AI slop, so it damages the brand they're trying to grow.

### Top 3 perceived problems

*What the avatar says when they complain. In their language, not the expert's. Three distinct domains.*

1. "Content takes so much time. I'm already running a business full-time, and content feels like another full-time job. I get consistent for a bit, then I fall off because I'm slammed with client work, then I come back. Back and forth, back and forth."
2. "I don't know what kind of content to make or how to think about it. My thumbnails suck, my titles don't work, and even when people click, they're gone in 30 seconds. My videos get low views. I feel like I'm stalling."
3. "I want to use AI to help, but everything it spits out is AI slop. My audience can tell it's not me, they trust me less, and my brand takes a hit."
