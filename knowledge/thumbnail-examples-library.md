# Thumbnail Examples Library

Annotated title and thumbnail combinations from real videos that performed. Each entry captures the hero element, why it works, and design notes derived from observation of the underlying example. Used by `vid-thumbnail` as calibration material. Find the closest example to the target video and match the SHAPE (hero choice, text energy, visual relationship), not the literal content.

This file is loaded by `vid-thumbnail` as a reference. Do not paste it into chat. Pull 1-3 closest examples when generating candidates.

## How to read each entry

```
Visual: brief description of the thumbnail image
Text on thumbnail: the exact words shown (in caps where applicable)
Title: the exact published title
Hero: what dominates the visual (the element doing the click work)
Why it works: the curiosity / belief-clash / proof mechanic at play
Note: design choices. What landed, what could be better, performance signal if known
```

## Terminology: categories used here vs. our system

The categories in this library map to the strategies in `knowledge/thumbnail-strategy-menu.md`:

| Library category | Strategy |
|---|---|
| Curiosity heavy (How / What / Why) | Curiosity |
| Comparison (Before/After) | Before/After Comparison |
| Result | Result |
| Social Hacking | Social Hacking |
| Cognitive Dissonance | Cognitive Dissonance (often invoked WITHIN curiosity examples) |

Several entries below sit at the overlap. A Curiosity-heavy thumbnail can also be Cognitive Dissonance (e.g. "BREAKFAST" with chocolate). Tag the dominant strategy.

## Category 4: Social Hacking

Borrowing recognized authority or fame to create instant credibility / curiosity.

### 1. "95% of your mind is controlled!": Celebrity expert on a major interview podcast

- **Visual:** Show logo top-left, famous-author guest on right, large text on left
- **Text on thumbnail:** 95% of your mind is controlled!
- **Title:** [Famous Expert]: You MUST Do This Before 10am To Fix It!
- **Hero:** Guest face plus text
- **Why it works:** The guest is a click magnet. Recognized power-person to the audience.
- **Note:** This style works for some channels but fails more often. It's easy, you can't just default to it. Must try other styles too unless this works over and over again.

### 2. "BEGINNER / EXPERT": Audio mixing

- **Visual:** Mixer pointing at frame, "BEGINNER" on red waveform left, "EXPERT" on green waveform right

- **Hero:** The man plus graphic
- **Why it works:** This guy is famous in his niche but not outside (300k views).
- **Note:** When social hacking, they need to be very famous to YOUR viewers. A bit famous won't work.

### 3. "OVER EDIT": Three globally famous YouTubers

- **Visual:** Three globally recognized YouTubers in a row, "OVER EDIT" white text top
- **Text on thumbnail:** OVER EDIT
- **Title:** Editing Hacks YouTubers Use To Hook You

## What to extract from this library

When generating candidates, scan this library for the closest analog by:

1. **Niche match**: fitness, business/finance, productivity, food/health, creator-economy
2. **Strategy match**: which of the 4 categories above (or combinations)
3. **Hero match**: text-dominant, number-dominant, face-plus-object, split-comparison, borrowed-celebrity

Then extract the SHAPE:

- **What's the hero element doing?** (a single word, a number, a contrast, a face)
- **What's the cognitive load?** (low equals instant, high equals too much to decode)
- **What's the curiosity gap?** (you want to know *why / how / what*)
- **What's the unusual element?** (the unexpected pairing: global pop celebrity on a vocal tricks video, $700k with a sad face, breakfast equals chocolate)

Apply the shape with the new video's actual material. Never copy the literal text or claims. Copy the mechanism.

## Lessons embedded in the notes (recurring patterns)

Across the 26 examples, a few repeated themes surface:

1. **Repeat what works.** When a thumbnail lands, base the channel style on it. The "FROM HERE → TO HERE" example became its activewear brand's default. 7 million views on the breakthrough.
2. **Also try new things.** Departures from a channel's house style frequently land big when the underlying contrast logic still holds (the anti-wrinkle and dopamine detox examples both broke from their channels' usual pattern and outperformed).
3. **Social hacking is a knife-edge.** It works when the borrowed person is FAMOUS to your viewers. A bit famous doesn't work. Going too wide with it brings the wrong audience to subsequent videos.
4. **Cognitive dissonance lets the image stay simple.** "LOOK POOR" works as text-only because the contradiction does the heavy lifting.
5. **Power words travel across niches.** "Lie," "Quit," "Stop," "Don't" get views in lots of places.
6. **Specificity wins.** $712,921.88 (to the cent), 275 → 175, -4.9KG, $7,500/week. Real numbers carry credibility.
7. **Positioning plus packaging is often the actual growth bottleneck.** The 6-hour positioning session that produced the "FROM HERE → TO HERE" thumbnail returned 7M views and reset the channel's permanent style. Fix packaging before assuming content is the problem.

These lessons should inform the planner's reasoning. Not as quotable rules but as judgment patterns when evaluating candidates.
