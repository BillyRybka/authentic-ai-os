# Transition Bank

Your channel's proven transitions. creator-setup seeds this file into your `banks/` as a starter; from then on it is yours. When a handoff you wrote keeps viewers through a section break, add it here. `vid-intro`, `vid-segment`, and `vid-ending` read this bank next to the plugin's transition pattern library and weight your winners heavier. A missing or empty bank is fine; the skills run on the plugin library alone.

The plugin's transition pattern library ([[transition-patterns]]) is the craft reference: the forward-hook rule, the fill-in-the-blank patterns per handoff, and the banned-phrase list in Section 4, which the skills check automatically. This bank is not a copy of it. This bank is your own proven winners, in your voice.

## Entry schema

One entry per proven transition:

- **Transition:** the line as you actually said it
- **Handoff:** intro-to-first-point | point-to-point | final-point-to-ending
- **Used in:** [[{the piece it ran in}]]
- **Receipt:** the signal that proves it held (no dip at the section break, viewers carried into the next beat)
- **Why it worked:** one line, your read

## Example entries (delete once you add your own)

- **Transition:** "That fix takes ten minutes. The next one took me two years to learn."
  - Handoff: point-to-point
  - Used in: [[first-minute-retention-video]]
  - Receipt: no dip at the segment 2 break
  - Why it worked: closes one payoff and opens a bigger one

- **Transition:** "You now have the full system. The catch: it only works if people click, and that is a different skill. I made a video on it."
  - Handoff: final-point-to-ending
  - Used in: [[the-one-email-video]]
  - Receipt: 62% reached the end screen
  - Why it worked: recaps in one line, then opens the next video's loop

## Growing this bank

- Add a transition only after it has run and held. Unproven ideas stay in the plugin's pattern library, not here.
- Every entry must forward-hook: raise a question, name a gap, or tease the next beat. A transition that just says "next" leaks retention.
- Run new entries past the banned-phrase list ([[transition-patterns]] Section 4) before adding them.
- Cut entries that stop landing.
