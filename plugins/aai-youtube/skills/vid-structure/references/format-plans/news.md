# News

Three beats, fast. What happened is the storytelling, why it matters is the parable, what to do is the principle. The whole body can run four minutes if the story is small.

## The body

```
## What happened
## Why it matters
## What to do
```

- Three sections, always, in this order. The body does not grow with more news; a second story is a second video.
- Speed rules the plan. Existing footage, screenshots, and the source itself. Nothing in the plan may wait on an asset that would blow the twenty-four hour window.
- The gut check is the planner's: if the reaction is "is there a story?", there is not one. Say so before planning.

## What each section locks

Every field is a label line with its beats under it. One beat per fact, per stake, per action.

What happened:

- **Facts:** who, what, when. One beat per fact, each with its source and date, marked confirmed, disputed, or unknown. An unsourced fact that changes the conclusion is a hole that breaks the format.

Why it matters:

- **Stakes:** what this changes for this avatar, specifically. Not "creators", this viewer.
- **POV:** the creator's take, the thing nobody else is saying. Without it the video is a press release.

What to do:

- **Actions:** the moves, or the watching position if the right move is wait. Each a verb, each tied to a stake above.

Parable and Principle fields are not used here. The three beats are the parable and the principle.

## Where the payoff lands

The why it matters beat. News compresses everything; the named answer cannot wait. One thread at most, and the useful fact goes first even when a later reveal would be more dramatic.

## Do not

- manufacture a late reveal
- plan a fact with no source
- plan a summary with no take
- plan a pitch; this format does not sell
- plan around b-roll that does not exist yet

## Example

Title: "YouTube just doubled mid-roll slots. Here is what changes for you"

```markdown
## What happened
**Facts:**
- videos over 8 minutes now get 6 automatic mid-roll slots, up from 4. YouTube Creator blog, 2 Sep, confirmed
- rollout to all monetised channels over two weeks. Same post, confirmed
- whether creators can cap the count: unknown, nothing in the post or the help centre

## Why it matters
**Stakes:**
- a 50 percent jump in ad inventory per video without touching anything
- a retention risk if the breaks land mid-sentence
- small channels feel the retention hit before they feel the revenue
**POV:** dump 03:20
- "everyone is going to talk about the money and nobody about the cuts"
- the creators who pre-place breaks win both

## What to do
**Actions:**
- audit the next three scripts for natural breaks at minutes 4, 6, and 8
- check the ad settings page when the rollout hits, look for a cap
- watch the retention graph on the first video that gets six
```
