# Post Brief Template

Fill this before writing each post. It is scaffolding for your thinking, not something the creator sees. A few words per line is enough.

- **Format (post-type):** mistake | story | framework | checklist | contrarian | warning | comparison | do-this-not-that
- **Platform:** linkedin | instagram-carousel | instagram-caption
- **Audience:** who exactly this is for, drawn from the avatar
- **Core belief:** what the reader should believe or do differently after reading
- **Enemy:** the behavior or belief this post pushes against
- **Proof:** the real story, number, example, or pattern from the creator's source. Never invented.
- **Practical takeaway:** the one thing they walk away able to do
- **CTA:** a specific ask, or none. No reflex sign-offs.
- **Hook type (planned):** contrarian | observation | story | diagnostic | rule | tension | command | comparison | question. Check it against what the batch has already used so the openings stay varied.

If you cannot fill **Proof** from the creator's real material, stop. Do not fabricate it. Flag the unit and move on. A thin unit is not a post yet.
