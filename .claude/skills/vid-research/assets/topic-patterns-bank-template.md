# Topic Patterns Bank

Topic clusters that pulled views in this audience. Sourced from own channel + niche channels ONLY. Adjacent niche topics never enter this bank — they don't transfer.

Loaded by `vid-framing` to surface 3-5 angle options for a specific video, anchored to topic clusters this audience has already shown they want more of. Loaded by `vid-intake` Mode 1 (idea + dump) to surface adjacent topics the creator may not have thought of.

## How to read this bank

Each cluster groups outliers covering related subject matter (not the same structural pattern — actual related content). Each cluster has:
- A descriptive label
- 3+ member outlier titles
- A hypothesis on why this topic works for this audience
- Confidence based on frequency

## Adjacent topics — DO NOT BANK

Per the Three-Circle Research rule: topics from adjacent niches don't transfer to this audience. Even if an adjacent niche has a high-performing topic cluster, it does NOT enter this bank.

What adjacent niches give us is title structures, power words, thumbnail patterns, formats — never topics. The yoga channel doesn't make weightlifting videos. It applies weightlifting THUMBNAIL composition to yoga topics.

If during research the AI surfaces a topic from an adjacent niche, flag and skip:
