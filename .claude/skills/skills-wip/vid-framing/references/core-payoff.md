# Core Payoff

The core payoff is what the viewer can do, decide, diagnose, or stop doing because they stayed.

It is not what the video covers.

It is not what the viewer learns or understands.

Weak:

> The viewer will understand why AI ignores writing rules.

Stronger:

> The viewer will be able to tell whether another prompt correction will hold or whether the output needs a different kind of check.

The stronger payoff ends in a decision the viewer can make.

## Derive it from the framing

Ask:

* What is the viewer unable to do reliably now?
* Where will they use the answer?
* What will they stop repeating?
* What can they decide once the video resolves its central question?

Write one outcome.

Good payoff verbs include:

* Choose
* Decide
* Diagnose
* Catch
* Fix
* Reject
* Build
* Tell
* Stop
* Use

Avoid ending on:

* Learn
* Understand
* Gain insight
* Feel confident
* Improve
* Become better

Those usually describe information or emotion without naming what changes.

## Four checks

**It lands on the fix.**
Knowing the cause is not enough. The viewer should be able to act differently.

**It reads clearly cold.**
A stranger should understand the outcome without seeing the framing.

**It does not reveal the conclusion.**
Promise the value of the answer, not the answer itself.

**It belongs to this video.**
It should not be equally true if the video reached a different conclusion.

## One outcome

Do not bolt on a second promise. It arrives as “and,” as a colon, as another sentence, and most often as a trailing “so” clause that restates the first half in softer words.

Weak:

> The viewer will know how to make the rule hold, and they will spend less time editing.

Weak:

> The viewer can hand off their own cleanup pass, so what lands on their screen has already been fixed.

Stronger:

> The viewer will know what has to change when repeating the rule no longer changes the output.

If the sentence has a comma in it, check what follows the comma. Usually it is the same promise a second time, and cutting it costs nothing.

The saved version flips from third person to second person. Nothing else changes.
