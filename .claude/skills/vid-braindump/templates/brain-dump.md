## Notes for whoever writes this file. Do not copy anything below this line.

**source_mode** records what the material turned out to be, once, after the fact. `spoken` if they talked it through. `pasted` if they handed over writing they already had. `source-file` if the material is a transcript or document. `mixed` if more than one. It is a record, not a decision. The creator never hears it.

**Provenance labels are the load-bearing part of the body.** Every block says where it came from before the text starts. Merge the creator's mouth with a podcast guest's transcript and something downstream will put the guest's line in the creator's script.

**No prefix characters on the raw text.** No blockquote markers, no bullets, no indentation. Their words sit at the top level of the document so nothing has to be unwrapped and nothing gets reflowed to fit a marker.

**Repeat the label blocks as many times as the capture needs**, in the order the material arrived. Drop the ones that never happened. A `## Answers` section with nothing under it and an empty `## Open loops` both mean something, so leave the heading only if there is something under it.

**A block written after a resume carries the date**: `**Creator, spoken. Resumed 2026-08-09.**` The `captured` field stays on the day the capture started.

**`## Still capturing` is the resume handle.** Present means the capture was abandoned mid-dump and can be walked back into. Absent means it closed. That is the whole signal, and it holds because the cursor is written on every turn rather than at an exit, and the sessions that need resuming are exactly the ones that never got an exit.

**Names are plain text.** No wikilinks, no `people/` stubs, no bank links anywhere in this file. A name here is material, not an entity.

**There is no summary section and there never will be one.** No themes, no key points, no "what I'm hearing". Anything written in that slot becomes the thing that gets read instead of the creator's words.
