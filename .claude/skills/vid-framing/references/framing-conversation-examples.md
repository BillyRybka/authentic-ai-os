# Framing Conversation Examples

Worked shapes for how a framing session runs. These describe the move and show the creator's side. They are not scripts to paste; write the AI line in your own words, fit to the moment. The italic notes are for the skill builder.

## Example 1: the read, then the yes/no gate (the heart)

**Creator:** "Ready to frame the ADHD planning video."

**AI move (lay back the read, then stop and wait):** reflect the one viewer, the main problem, the transformation, and the core payoff in a few plain lines, then ask "is that the video, or do you want to sharpen the problem or the transformation?" Do not propose an angle yet.

What the read covers:
- Viewer: the one person who clicks this, from the avatar, localized to this topic.
- Main problem: the thing they are actually stuck on (not the surface topic).
- Transformation: what changes for them by the end.
- Core payoff: what they walk away able to do.

**Creator:** "Yeah, but the problem is more that they think planning harder will fix it, when the issue is the system fights their brain."

**AI move:** adjust the read to their wording, confirm the core payoff in one line, then move to grounding.

*Note: the gate is the point. A whole session aimed at the wrong problem is the expensive mistake. Build nothing until they say "that's it."*

## Example 2: hunt the reframe, then ground it

**AI move (only after the confirm):** find the angle that makes the idea feel new (one of the five moves in `reframe-toolkit.md`, judged against BENS, weighting N), then pull the pattern bank and name the real pattern behind each. Shape one or two angles that deliver the confirmed payoff and press the tension.

- **Angle:** "Stop Planning Harder, Your System Is Fighting Your ADHD Brain." Grounded in the STOP pattern, proven by "STOP Planning Your Week Like This" (@CoachY, 800k). Backed by the creator's own burnout story in the brain-dump. Risk: needs a strong alternative in the body. *This one came out headline-shaped, and that is allowed. Do not polish it into a title here; it goes to vid-title as one candidate, free to beat.*
- **Gut swing (no pattern):** "I Threw Out My Calendar." Flagged as the gut pick, no data behind it, higher variance.

Each angle gets a one-line "why it could land" and a one-line risk. The creator picks.

### Near-miss: hand-waving instead of grounding

> "STOP titles work in your niche. Curiosity hooks get clicks. Your audience would relate."

No named outlier, no number, no channel. The creator cannot weigh it. Either name the real entry or call the angle a gut pick. See `angle-anchor-rules.md`.

### Near-miss: building before the yes

Surfacing four angles right after reading the brain-dump, before the creator confirmed the problem and the transformation. If the read is wrong, every angle is wrong. Always run the gate first.

## Example 3: re-frame

**Context:** piece.md already has a `selected_angle`.

**AI move:** surface the existing angle, ask "re-frame from scratch, or refine this one?" Do not resurface previously dropped angles unless the creator asks for them.

## Example 4: thin bank

**AI move:** if the pattern bank has nothing real for this topic, say so plainly, offer to run vid-research first, or shape the angle from the material alone and flag it as ungrounded. Never invent an outlier to fill the gap.
