# Deep dive format card

Fourteen numbered blanks, in the order the file lays them out and the order they get asked. Blanks 9, 10, and 11 repeat once per principle step, so the real count is 11 plus 3 per step.

`knowledge/format-planners/deep-dive.md` is the content source of truth. This card holds the wording of each ask and the bar a passing answer clears, and points back at the planner for everything else. Nothing from the planner gets restated here. Two copies of one rule drift the first time either gets edited.

**Load-bearing: blanks 5, 7, and 12.** The planner gives each one a dedicated statement that the video fails without it: the filter ("More overwhelms", and What to skip says apply it ruthlessly), the proof block ("nobody sits through 60 minutes without first seeing evidence the time is worth spending"), and the close (its own "Critical caveat" heading, naming the no-pitch deep dive as a known failure mode). Those three get up to two follow-ups when the answer comes back thin, then a gap callout. Every other blank gets one ask and takes whatever comes back.

**Section names in the plan file are the blank names below**, numbered as they are numbered here.

**One thing per ask.** Some blanks hold two things and carry an `Ask 2`. That is two short turns, not one long question. The count in the progress line does not move between them.

**Blank 5 sets the step count.** Until it lands, the total in the progress line carries a `+`. See the fan-out rule in `SKILL.md`.

## 1. Goal

- **Section:** `## 1. Goal`
- **Planner:** step 1 of the 11-step planner
- **Load-bearing:** no
- **Ask:** Sales, emails, or views?
- **Push:** none.
- **Passing:** one goal named.
- **Notes:** usually answered at step 0. The planner splits the recommendation by channel size: sales for established creators, views for small or new channels. Say that once if they are stuck, then take their call.

## 2. Title and thumbnail

- **Section:** `## 2. Title and thumbnail`
- **Planner:** step 2
- **Load-bearing:** no
- **Ask:** Working title?
- **Ask 2:** What's on the thumbnail?
- **Push:** none.
- **Passing:** a title in words plus what the thumbnail shows.
- **Notes:** the planner is blunt that overpromising here tanks finish-rate, and that general-claim titles are a common mistake. Do not rewrite their title. If the promise is bigger than what blank 5 leaves behind, raise it once at the close.

## 3. Viewer's top questions

- **Section:** `## 3. Viewer's top questions`
- **Planner:** step 3
- **Load-bearing:** no
- **Ask:** How long is this one, roughly?
- **Ask 2:** What questions does that package put in their head?
- **Push:** none.
- **Passing:** three questions under 45 minutes, five at 45 and over.
- **Notes:** the count is not decoration. The planner raises it to five for long videos because a 45-minute video needs more anticipation to hold. Ask the length first so you know which count to hold them to.

## 4. Brain dump: every step and its proof

- **Section:** `## 4. Brain dump: every step and its proof`
- **Planner:** step 4
- **Load-bearing:** no
- **Ask:** Every step in the system. All of them, no order.
- **Ask 2:** Which ones can you prove worked?
- **Push:** none.
- **Passing:** a long unordered list, plus whatever proof they can name against it. Long is correct here. Blank 5 does the cutting.
- **Notes:** do not tidy the list or merge steps as it comes in. The overlap they leave is what blank 5 works on.

## 5. The cut

- **Section:** `## 5. The cut`
- **Planner:** step 5, the 20-from-100 filter
- **Load-bearing:** yes
- **Ask:** Which of those actually produce the result?
- **Push 1:** If they could only do five of them, which five?
- **Push 2:** Which one could you drop and nobody would notice?
- **Passing:** a shorter numbered list, in delivery order, where every step earns its place. The planner's bar: of 100 possible steps, the 20 easiest to action that produce the biggest result.
- **Notes:** this blank sets the step count for blanks 9 through 11. Write the list to the file, then write one section per step before the next question goes out. The creator who says "keep them all" gets both pushes and then a gap callout, because the planner names including everything as the mistake that guarantees the audience can't action any of it. The bar for the list that survives: a plan that would make a viewer comment they can't believe this is free.

## 6. Intro: the setup contract

- **Section:** `## 6. Intro: the setup contract`
- **Planner:** the Intro adaptation table, rows 1 and 4
- **Load-bearing:** no
- **Ask:** What does the intro promise they'll walk out with?
- **Push:** none.
- **Passing:** three promises under 45 minutes, five at 45 and over, each one a thing the video actually delivers.
- **Notes:** the planner calls the setup the contract. Check it against blank 5 as it lands: a promise with no step behind it is the overpromise the planner warns about. Name the mismatch once, do not fix it for them.

## 7. The proof block

- **Section:** `## 7. The proof block`
- **Planner:** step 6, and the structure section
- **Load-bearing:** yes
- **Ask:** What proof goes in the first 30 seconds after the intro?
- **Push 1:** Whose results? Name them.
- **Push 2:** What's on screen while you say it?
- **Passing:** named clients, numbers, or before and afters that can be shown, enough to fill roughly 30 seconds.
- **Notes:** this is the block the planner says only deep dive has, and the reason is that nobody commits an hour on faith. Check `banks/proof-bank/` for entries that already cover it and link them as `[[proof-bank/{slug}]]` when the file is there.

## 8. The parable

- **Section:** `## 8. The parable`
- **Planner:** step 7
- **Load-bearing:** no
- **Ask:** What's the old way you're arguing against?
- **Ask 2:** What's the new way?
- **Push:** none.
- **Passing:** a status quo stated with conviction, and the thing that replaces it.
- **Notes:** the planner names Old Way / New Way as the common deep dive parable and gives its four beats. If the creator's material is a story or a visual demo instead, take that. The planner says commonly, not always.

## 9. Step {n}: what it is

- **Section:** `### 9.{n} Step {n}: what it is`
- **Planner:** step 8
- **Load-bearing:** no
- **Repeats:** once per step, count set by blank 5
- **Ask:** Step {n}. What do they actually do?
- **Push:** none.
- **Passing:** an action, specific enough to follow without you there.

## 10. Step {n}: the proof

- **Section:** `### 10.{n} Step {n}: the proof`
- **Planner:** step 8, and the "Proof woven throughout" note
- **Load-bearing:** no
- **Repeats:** once per step, count set by blank 5
- **Ask:** What proves that one works?
- **Push:** none.
- **Passing:** a client win, a before and after, or a number tied to this step.
- **Notes:** the planner's ideal pattern is step, proof, step, proof. Each one is a line and a visual, not a nested case study; quick is part of what makes it work. Empty on a step or two is survivable. Empty on all of them is worth naming once at the close.

## 11. Step {n}: does it need a parable

- **Section:** `### 11.{n} Step {n}: does it need a parable`
- **Planner:** step 8
- **Load-bearing:** no
- **Repeats:** once per step, count set by blank 5
- **Ask:** Will they believe this one, or does it need a story?
- **Push:** none.
- **Passing:** a named story, metaphor, or visual demo, or a clear no.
- **Notes:** the planner is explicit that parables do not only go at the start. Most steps answer no and that is the correct answer. Ask it once per step and move fast.

## 12. The close

- **Section:** `## 12. The close`
- **Planner:** step 9, and the Critical caveat
- **Load-bearing:** yes
- **Ask:** set by the goal in blank 1.
  - **Sales:** What's the ask at the end?
  - **Sales 2:** Anything woven through as you name the offer?
  - **Emails:** What's the lead magnet? The planner wants the shortcut version of the system.
  - **Emails 2:** Where do the three mentions land?
  - **Views:** No links on this one. Anything at the end instead?
- **Push 1:** Say the line. What comes out of your mouth at minute 58?
- **Push 2:** What's the link, and where does it sit in the description?
- **Passing:** a stated ask plus the description decision. For views, "no links" is a complete answer and the pushes do not run.
- **Notes:** the planner's Critical caveat names the no-pitch deep dive as a known failure mode, because the viewer who finished an hour IS the buyer. Hold the push on sales and emails. On views there is nothing to push for.

## 13. Delivery

- **Section:** `## 13. Delivery`
- **Planner:** step 10, and the Output format section
- **Load-bearing:** no
- **Ask:** Word for word, or are you planning to wing parts of it?
- **Push:** none.
- **Passing:** word for word, which is what the planner wants, or their own call recorded as theirs.
- **Notes:** the planner says filming from bullets is not recommended and names it first in Common mistakes. Say that once if they want bullets, then write down what they decide.

## 14. Upload and track

- **Section:** `## 14. Upload and track`
- **Planner:** step 11, and the End screen routing section
- **Load-bearing:** no
- **Ask:** Which video does the end screen point at?
- **Ask 2:** What number tells you this one worked?
- **Push:** none.
- **Passing:** a real published title and one metric tied to the goal in blank 1.
- **Notes:** the planner routes the end screen by goal. A video they intend to make is a gap, not an answer.
