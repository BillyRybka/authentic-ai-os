# Listicle format card

Twelve numbered blanks, in the order the file lays them out and the order they get asked. Blanks 7, 8, and 9 repeat once per point, so the real count is 9 plus 3 per point.

`knowledge/format-planners/listicle.md` is the content source of truth. This card holds the wording of each ask and the bar a passing answer clears, and points back at the planner for everything else. Nothing from the planner gets restated here. Two copies of one rule drift the first time either gets edited.

**Load-bearing: blank 9, the transition out of each point.** It is the one thing the planner gives a dedicated section to and names as fatal: weak transitions kill retention, and a transition that is just an announcement makes viewers drop. Nothing else in this doc is stated that way, so nothing else is marked. That blank gets up to two follow-ups when the answer comes back thin, then a gap callout. Every other blank gets one ask and takes whatever comes back.

**Section names in the plan file are the blank names below**, numbered as they are numbered here.

**One thing per ask.** Some blanks hold two things and carry an `Ask 2`. That is two short turns, not one long question. The count in the progress line does not move between them.

**Blank 5 sets the point count.** Until it lands, the total in the progress line carries a `+`. See the fan-out rule in `SKILL.md`.

## 1. Goal

- **Section:** `## 1. Goal`
- **Planner:** step 1 of the 10-step planner
- **Load-bearing:** no
- **Ask:** Views, emails, or sales?
- **Push:** none.
- **Passing:** one goal named.
- **Notes:** usually answered at step 0. The planner recommends views, with emails second and sales as the alternative when one strong point connects to an offer. It also carries an audience-fit caveat: listicles land for some channels and tank for others. If the creator has not run one before, say that once and move.

## 2. Title and thumbnail

- **Section:** `## 2. Title and thumbnail`
- **Planner:** step 2
- **Load-bearing:** no
- **Ask:** Working title?
- **Ask 2:** What's on the thumbnail?
- **Push:** none.
- **Passing:** a title in words, ideally carrying the count, plus what the thumbnail shows.
- **Notes:** the planner says the count is the format's contract and belongs in the thumbnail. If their title has a number in it, that number is the target for blank 5 and you carry it there.

## 3. Viewer's top 3 questions

- **Section:** `## 3. Viewer's top 3 questions`
- **Planner:** step 3
- **Load-bearing:** no
- **Ask:** What three questions does that package put in their head?
- **Push:** none.
- **Passing:** three questions, phrased as questions.
- **Notes:** the planner names the three that come up most for listicles. Offer them as candidates only after the creator has taken their own swing.

## 4. Brain dump: every point you might use

- **Section:** `## 4. Brain dump: every point you might use`
- **Planner:** step 4
- **Load-bearing:** no
- **Ask:** Every point you might include. No order, no filter.
- **Push:** none.
- **Passing:** a long unordered list. The planner wants 1.5 to 2 times the final count so blank 5 has room to cut.
- **Notes:** if they stop at exactly the number in their title, say once that the cut works better with more to cut from, then take what they give.

## 5. The cut to the final count

- **Section:** `## 5. The cut to the final count`
- **Planner:** step 5
- **Load-bearing:** no
- **Ask:** Which ones survive?
- **Push:** none.
- **Passing:** the final numbered list, in running order.
- **Notes:** the planner's test is whether the average viewer in the avatar would action it. Apply that as the question if they stall, not as a lecture. This blank sets the point count for blanks 7 through 9: write the list to the file, then write one section per point before the next question goes out. The planner also warns against padding the count to hit the number in the title. If they are two short, say so once and let them decide whether to cut the title or find two more.

## 6. Intro: the count and the payoff

- **Section:** `## 6. Intro: the count and the payoff`
- **Planner:** the Intro adaptation table, rows 2 and 4
- **Load-bearing:** no
- **Ask:** What do they have by the end of all of them?
- **Push:** none.
- **Passing:** the cumulative payoff in one line. The count itself comes from blank 5.
- **Notes:** the planner says the count is the setup contract and the hook is often the count itself. The worked intro example in the doc is there if they want to see one. Do not write their opening.

## 7. Point {n}: the parable

- **Section:** `### 7.{n} Point {n}: the parable`
- **Planner:** step 6, and the parable decision matrix
- **Load-bearing:** no
- **Repeats:** once per point, count set by blank 5
- **Ask:** Point {n}. How do you show it? Demo, story, contrast, breakdown, or metaphor.
- **Push:** none.
- **Passing:** one type named, plus the material behind it (the thing being demoed, the story, the two sides of the contrast).
- **Notes:** run the planner's matrix silently against what the point is and offer the type it lands on as a candidate. Do not read the matrix at them. Track the types as they accumulate: the planner says reusing one type across every point makes the video flat, so if points 1 through 4 are all stories, say so once at point 5.

## 8. Point {n}: the principle

- **Section:** `### 8.{n} Point {n}: the principle`
- **Planner:** step 7
- **Load-bearing:** no
- **Repeats:** once per point, count set by blank 5
- **Ask:** What's the lesson they take from it?
- **Push:** none.
- **Passing:** one lesson, small enough to land in a minute or two.
- **Notes:** the planner warns against burying a 20-step process inside one point. If the answer arrives as a process, say once that it might be its own video, then write down whatever they want to keep.

## 9. Point {n}: the transition out

- **Section:** `### 9.{n} Point {n}: the transition out`
- **Planner:** the Transitions are the lifeline section
- **Load-bearing:** yes
- **Repeats:** once per point, count set by blank 5
- **Ask:** Why should they stay for the next one?
- **Push 1:** What does the next point get them? Say it as the result.
- **Push 2:** Finish this: "Point {n+1} is the one that..."
- **Passing:** a promise of a result the viewer wants, not an announcement of a number. The planner's split: "Lesson number one" is weak, "lesson number one is gonna get you more consistent sales and leads" is strong.
- **Notes:** this is the only load-bearing blank in the format and it repeats, so the pushes run per point. That gets tiring fast, and the fix is that the first two or three teach the shape. Once their answers are landing, they usually keep landing. The last point transitions into the ending, so ask it as what pays off for staying to the end.

## 10. Conversion

- **Section:** `## 10. Conversion`
- **Planner:** step 8, and the matching subsection of Conversion strategy by goal
- **Load-bearing:** no
- **Ask:** set by the goal in blank 1.
  - **Views:** No links on this one. Anything at the end instead?
  - **Emails:** What's the lead magnet?
  - **Emails 2:** Which points do the three mentions sit after?
  - **Sales:** Which point is strong enough to put the ask right after it?
  - **Sales 2:** What's the top link in the description?
- **Push:** none.
- **Passing:** the form of the ask plus the description decision. For views, "no links" is a complete answer.
- **Notes:** the planner puts the mid-video ask right after a strong point, while the emotional high is fresh. The lead magnet it suggests for this format is the list itself as a cheat sheet.

## 11. Delivery

- **Section:** `## 11. Delivery`
- **Planner:** step 9, and the Output format section
- **Load-bearing:** no
- **Ask:** Word for word?
- **Ask 2:** Chapter cards on screen between points?
- **Push:** none.
- **Passing:** the shooting shape plus a yes or no on the cards.
- **Notes:** the planner wants text chapter cards between points so viewers know where they are in the list. Ask it, do not insist.

## 12. Upload and track

- **Section:** `## 12. Upload and track`
- **Planner:** step 10, and the End screen routing section
- **Load-bearing:** no
- **Ask:** Which video does the end screen point at?
- **Ask 2:** What number tells you this one worked?
- **Push:** none.
- **Passing:** a real published title and one metric tied to the goal in blank 1.
- **Notes:** the planner routes views to another listicle for the binge, and the other two goals to a past converter. A video they intend to make is a gap, not an answer.
